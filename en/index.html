Privacy Policy

This notice covers the ad-supported RUVOKSA Android game version 1.0.5 (versionCode 9) and its data processing practices.

Updated: November 26, 2026com.ruvoksa.game

1. Application and contact

This policy covers the RUVOKSA Android game with package name com.ruvoksa.game. For privacy questions and support, contact kivobrix.support@gmail.com. This is also a support contact channel for RUVOKSA. Do not post personal information in public GitHub areas.

2. Local gameplay records

The game may store scores, high scores, gameplay progress, achievements, and settings on the device. Android backup services may separately process local records depending on device settings.

3. Offline play and accounts

The current core game does not require an internet connection or a RUVOKSA-specific online player account. The developer does not currently operate a separate player-account server.

4. Advertising and third parties

RUVOKSA uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve live ads and support game development. Players can choose to watch rewarded ads to continue a run, earn gold or receive bonus gifts. Occasional interstitial ads may also be shown after a run ends. There are no mandatory ads during gameplay and no banner ads. Ad loading depends on internet connectivity and ad availability.

Google and relevant advertising service providers may collect and share IP addresses (which may be used to estimate approximate location), Android advertising IDs (AAID) and other device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information. These data may be processed for ad delivery, measurement and analytics, security and fraud prevention. Data processing and ad types may vary depending on device settings, region, age-safety configuration and applicable privacy choices.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from partner apps and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

5. Consent and children's privacy

RUVOKSA uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising-related privacy and consent status. A privacy or consent message may be shown depending on the region and applicable requirements. Users can manage their preferences through the options presented. Android advertising settings may also allow users to reset or delete their advertising ID and manage ad preferences, depending on device support. These options may not remove all ads.

RUVOKSA is intended for players of different age groups. Protection of children and users under the age of consent is taken into account in the advertising configuration. For privacy or children's privacy questions, contact the support address above.

6. Deletion of local records

Users can clear locally stored game data in Android app settings or uninstall the app. Device and cloud backups may follow the retention rules of the relevant provider.

7. Support and privacy rights

Information voluntarily sent in support requests may be used to respond to the request. For applicable access, correction, and deletion requests, contact kivobrix.support@gmail.com.

8. Policy changes

This notice will be reviewed if game permissions, online services, SDKs, or data handling change; the update date will be revised accordingly.
