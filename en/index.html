Privacy Policy

This notice covers the currently tested base build of RUVOKSA. Advertising technology has not yet been integrated.

Updated: October 31, 2026com.ruvoksa.game

1. Application and contact

This policy covers the RUVOKSA Android game with package name com.ruvoksa.game. The developer can be reached through the GitHub profile. Do not post personal information in public GitHub areas. A dedicated privacy contact address will be provided before store publication.

2. Local gameplay records

The game may store scores, high scores, gameplay progress, achievements, and settings on the device. Android backup services may separately process local records depending on device settings.

3. Offline play and accounts

The current core game does not require an internet connection or a RUVOKSA-specific online player account. The developer does not currently operate a separate player-account server.

4. Advertising and third parties

As of October 31, 2026, the tested build does not integrate AdMob or another advertising SDK. If ads are added in the future, providers may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. Future ad-supported releases will be assessed for applicable consent, age-safety, and Google Play audience requirements. Technologies such as Google UMP are not presented as active features before they have been integrated.

6. Deletion of local records

Users can clear locally stored game data in Android app settings or uninstall the app. Device and cloud backups may follow the retention rules of the relevant provider.

7. Support and privacy rights

Information voluntarily sent in support requests may be used to respond to the request. Direct contact details for applicable access, correction, and deletion rights will be finalized before store publication.

8. Policy changes

This notice will be reviewed if game permissions, online services, SDKs, or data handling change; the update date will be revised accordingly.
